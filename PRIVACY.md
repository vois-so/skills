# Privacy

The skills in this repository operate on your computer. The skills and their scripts do not send data to a server.

## What the skills read

- The text, scripts and files that you give to the agent for the task.
- The status, the voice list and the plan of Vois Studio, from vois-cli on your computer.

## What the skills do with the data

- They give the text to vois-cli on your computer. Vois Studio makes the audio on your computer.
- Some scripts use other tools on your computer: ffmpeg, ffprobe and a transcription tool for the transcript check.
- The skills write the audio files, captions, manifests and reports into the folder of your project. Only you select where these files go after that.

## Tools from other companies

Some skills tell the agent to use tools from other companies, for example Remotion or HyperFrames through `npx`. These tools download their packages from the internet. Each tool has its own privacy policy.

## Vois Studio

Vois Studio has its own [privacy policy](https://vois.so/legal/privacy).
